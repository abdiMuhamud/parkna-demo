04 — Prices

One price for every plate

Paid hours are 7am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday; parking is free at other times. A warning is paid before any new pass. The full rules are in the terms and conditions.

Daily pass200 GMDValid till 7pm on the day you pay.
Monthly pass4,000 GMDA month of parking for the price of 20 days.
Fine1,880 GMDAn unpaid car gets a warning: pay the daily fee within 24 hours, or the fine is added.
Organisations48,000 GMDPer car, per year, paid upfront on one invoice.
05 — Parking attendants

The SUNU Park Officer app, or SMS

For attendants registered in the back office. From the registered number, text START to begin a shift, a plate like BJL1234 to check it and END to finish, or dial *7275#. Attendants never take money.
