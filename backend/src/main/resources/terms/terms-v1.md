## 1. About SUNU Park
SUNU Park ("our park" or "our parking") is the street parking service of Banjul City Council. It is run for the Council by INNOVII. These terms apply to everyone who parks in a marked SUNU Park bay, uses the SUNU Park app, the SMS line or the organisation portal. By using SUNU Park you accept them.

## 2. Paid hours and bays
- Parking in a marked SUNU Park bay is paid from 7am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday. Sundays and evenings are free.
- A pass follows the plate, not the phone or the person who paid. You can pay for any plate.
- Attendants check plates and never take cash.

## 3. Fees
SUNU Park has three fee models. Prices are set by Banjul City Council and shown in the app and by SMS before you pay.
- **Hourly:** pay for the hours you park. Not offered yet: the Council will announce when it starts.
- **Daily:** 200 GMD, valid until 7pm the same day.
- **Monthly:** 4,000 GMD for 30 days (20 paid days: you pay for 5 days a week instead of 6). It can be renewed up to 3 days before it ends.
- **Organisations:** pay upfront for a year for every car on their account. Cars added later pay for the months left.

## 4. Paying
- Pay in the app or by texting your plate to the SUNU Park short code, with Wave, Afrimoney, APS or QMoney.
- Every payment gets a ticket number and a receipt by SMS. Keep it until the pass ends.
- Check the plate before you pay: a payment for a wrong plate is handled as an exception by the Council.

## 5. Warnings and fines
- A car parked in a SUNU Park bay during paid hours without a valid pass gets a warning, by SMS and in the app.
- The daily fee (200 GMD) must be paid within 24 hours of the warning.
- After 24 hours, a fine of 1,880 GMD for rule breakers is added (2,080 GMD in total).
- Unpaid warnings and fines are settled first, before any new daily or monthly payment for any plate on your account.
- Warnings can also be paid at the Council office.
- A record of warnings is kept for each plate. Attendants and the police can see whether a car is a first-time or a repeat offender, and the police can follow up unpaid fines.

## 6. Your account and your data
- You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS. Never share the code.
- SUNU Park keeps your number, name, plates, payments, warnings and messages to run the service. See the privacy policy for details.

## 7. Changes to these terms
The Council can change these terms and the fees. A new version is shown in the app, which asks you to read and accept it, and is announced by SMS. The current version is always at sunupark.gm/terms and at the Council office.

## 8. Contact
Banjul City Council, SUNU Park office. Questions and complaints: through the app's help page or at the Council office.
